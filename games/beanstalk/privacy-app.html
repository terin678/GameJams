Beanstalk and your privacy

Beanstalk does not ask who you are, and has no accounts, no advertising and no purchases inside the game.

Your farm stays on your device

Your progress and settings (sound, music, the farm view) are saved on the device you play on, and nowhere else. Nobody else can see them. Clearing the app's data, or choosing "Start over", erases them.

Nothing is sent, unless you switch on Play Games

The app counts nothing and sends nothing by itself, and it works with no connection at all.

There is one thing you can switch on: Google Play Games, in the Menu under Achievements. If you do, the achievements you have earned and your best scores are sent to Google Play Games under your Google account, so that they show on Play's own lists. Nothing else is sent. Google handles that under its own privacy policy. Switch it off in the Menu at any time and the app goes back to sending nothing. This is the only reason the app has Android's permission to use the internet.

If you switch on the reminder in the Menu, the app shows one notification when the farm has done all it can without you. It is made on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere to do it. Switch it off in the Menu at any time.

Reporting a problem

"Report a problem" in the Menu writes a short report on your device: how far your farm has got, what kind of phone or browser you are using, and any errors the game hit. It has nothing in it that says who you are. It is not sent anywhere unless you copy it and post it yourself. The button beside it opens the game's Discord server, which is run by Discord and has its own privacy policy.
