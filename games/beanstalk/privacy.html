Beanstalk and your privacy

Beanstalk does not ask who you are, and has no accounts, no advertising and no purchases inside the game.

Your farm stays on your device

Your progress and settings (sound, music, the farm view) are saved on the device you play on, and nowhere else. Nobody else can see them. Clearing the site's or the app's data, or choosing "Start over", erases them.

The website

The website version counts visits with GoatCounter, so that we know how many people play. It sets no cookies, does not follow you to other sites, and keeps nothing that identifies you. The bug and idea links open GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.

The phone app

The app counts nothing and sends nothing. It works with no connection at all.
