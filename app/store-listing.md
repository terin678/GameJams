# Store listing: draft text and form answers

Drafts for the Play Console. Edit freely; nothing reads this file.

## Main listing

- **App name** (30 characters at most): Beanstalk
- **Short description** (80 at most): Plant one bean. Keep going. An idle farm that grows past the clouds.
- **Full description:**

  You have one plot, one bean and a watering can.

  Beanstalk is a quiet idle game about a farm that gets out of hand. Plant,
  pick and sell. Hire a farmhand. Make friends with the baker. Breed a better
  bean and take it to the county fair. Post ducks against the slugs. Send
  someone up the stalk to see who lives in the clouds.

  Then keep going.

  - A whole game in a few hours, with an ending
  - Plays itself while you are away, and tells you what happened
  - New things to do arrive one at a time, as you are ready for them
  - A pixel farm that changes with every choice, season and hour of the day
  - Calm music, no ads, no accounts, nothing to buy inside the game
  - Works with no connection

- **Category:** Games, Simulation (or Casual)
- **Developer name:** Veracity
- **Privacy policy:** https://terin678.github.io/GameJams/games/beanstalk/privacy.html
  (move it when the game leaves the free site)
